Dorker - Quick Start (Windows)

No WSL, no install needed. Just this folder.

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (e.g. your Desktop).

2. Double-click  Dorker-Panel.bat
   - A small black window opens and your web browser opens the control panel.
   - First time, Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC"
     because the app isn't code-signed. Click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
     (Same reason your antivirus may ask - it's an unsigned new program.)

3. In the panel (your browser):
   - Setup (optional): paste your Google API key + search-engine id, click Save,
     to turn on auto-discovery. Leave blank to skip.
   - Start: find & scan.  Or paste one link into "Scan one site".
   - Click any result to open its full colored report.
   - "Stop after N positives" box: collect a set batch, then it stops.

4. To stop: click "Quit panel" in the browser, or close the black window.

What the scores mean:
    1-25  Very unlikely      76-85  Likely
   26-50  Unlikely           86-90  Very likely
   51-75  Possible           91-100 Almost certain

For authorized anti-fraud research. Dorker reads public pages politely -
it never logs in, attacks, or submits anything.
